Wedding RSVP — Google Sheet layout
==================================

Row 1 headers (columns A–I):

  A: Timestamp
  B: Name
  C: Email
  D: Attending        (values: yes / no — do not change spelling)
  E: Guests           (number; 0 when not attending)
  F: Recovery dinner  (yes / no / blank)
  G: Responses        (live total — formula below)
  H: Attendees        (live total — formula below)
  I: Rejections       (live total — formula below)

Dynamic totals (recommended)
----------------------------
Put these three formulas once in row 2 (cells G2, H2, I2). Leave A2:F2 empty
until the first RSVP arrives, or keep the formulas in G2:I2 on the same row
as your first guest — they will keep updating as new rows are added below.

  G2  =COUNTA(FILTER(B2:B, B2:B<>""))
  H2  =SUM(FILTER(E2:E, D2:D="yes"))
  I2  =COUNTIF(D2:D, "no")

These ranges (B2:B, D2:D, E2:E) grow with every new submission, so the totals
are always up to date — no need to copy formulas down.

What they mean:
  Responses   — number of RSVPs submitted (rows with a name)
  Attendees   — total people coming (sum of Guests where Attending = yes)
  Rejections  — number of “no” replies

Optional: pin row 1 (View → Freeze → 1 row) so headers stay visible.

Timestamp column
----------------
New rows get a readable local time from the server, e.g.:
  Wed, 21 Oct 2026, 11:25
(Argentina / Buenos Aires timezone)

Older rows may still show ISO format (2026-10-21T…); you can leave them or
reformat manually. New submissions after redeploy use the friendly format.

Netlify
-------
  GOOGLE_SHEET_RANGE=Sheet1!A:F

Change Sheet1 if your tab name is different.

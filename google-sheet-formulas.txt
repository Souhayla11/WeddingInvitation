Wedding RSVP — Google Sheet layout
==================================

Row 1 headers (columns A–I):

  A: Timestamp
  B: Name
  C: Email
  D: Attending        (values: yes / no — do not change spelling)
  E: Guests           (number; 0 when not attending)
  F: Recovery dinner  (yes / no / blank)
  G: Responses        (TOTAL — formula below, not filled by the website)
  H: Attendees        (TOTAL — formula below)
  I: Rejections       (TOTAL — formula below)

Put these formulas in row 2 (same row as your first RSVP is fine; G–I stay as formulas):

  G2  =COUNTA(B2:B)
  H2  =SUMIF(D2:D,"yes",E2:E)
  I2  =COUNTIF(D2:D,"no")

What they mean:
  Responses   — how many people submitted the form (rows with a name)
  Attendees   — total guest count (sum of Guests where Attending = yes)
  Rejections  — how many RSVPs chose no

The Netlify function only writes columns A–F on each new row. Columns G–I are never overwritten.

Netlify env (optional):
  GOOGLE_SHEET_RANGE=Sheet1!A:F

If your tab is not named Sheet1, change the name in that range.
